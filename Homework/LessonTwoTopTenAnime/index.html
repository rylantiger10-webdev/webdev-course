My top fifteen anime

My name is Rylan Maxfield, and this is a list of top 15 anime i've watched

  1. My next life as a Villainess: All routes lead to Doom!

    2. This anime kickstarted the "Villaness" sub-genre. It's about a highschool girl who died in a car crash and got reincarnated into the Villainess of the Otome game she played. She comes up with several ways to keep her for being exiled or killed, and through these plans, her personality, and a bit of clumsy luck, she ends up having every major character of the Otome game fall in love with her, without her noticing.

  2. Love after world domination!

    3. A personal favorite of mine, this anime portrays a forbidden love between a hero and a villian based of the Power Rangers. It is a great mix of comedy and romance, with the action scenes mixing well with the overall plot.

  3. An observation log of my fiance who calls herself a villainess

    4. This anime is a about the observations of a price watching his fiance who was reincarnated into the role of the villainess. The plot is hard to summarise without spoiling anything. It is has a interesting a unique plot and uses plot points well.

  4. Heroine? Saint? No, I'm an all works maid!

    5. This anime is a fun twist on the Otome game sub-genre. The main character was reincarnated into a Otome game without playing or knowing about the game. She was reicarnated as the heroine, and worked on her dream of becoming a maid. she quickly mastered her magic, and uses it for maid work.

  5. Pass the monster meat, Milady!

    6. This anime is about a girl who cooks and eats monsters. In this world, monsters have a special poison, which she extracts so she can eat the meat. It is a fun action romance with a good plot.

  6. Kakuriyo - Bed and Breakfast for spirits

    7. This anime is about a girl who can see spirts. When she comes of age, she is whisked away to the spirit realm to settle her grandfather's debt. To pay for the debt, she opens a restaurant with food from the appearant realm.

  7. Campfire cooking in another world

    8. This anime follows someone summoned to another world with the skill Online Grocery. While leaving the kingdom he was summoned in, he encounters a fenrir which he "tames" after giving it some of his food that he made using Online Grocery. It's a fun lighthearted action comedy.

  8. Kaju girl Caramalise

    9. This new anime is a perfect example of romance with a twist. It follows a girl who turns into a kaiju when feeling strong emotions. It follows her as she deals with navigating a romantic relationship. It mixes the nuances of a relationship quite well with kaiju transformations and references.

  9. The Royal Tutor

    10. This anime follows the royal tutor as he navigates 4 princes, each with their own issues. He uses a different style of teaching to accommodate each prince. It's fairly detailed and realistic, and a very good watch.

  10. By the grace of the gods

    11. A good "feel good" anime, this anime follows a overworked corporate slave's reincarnation into another world. It goes through his journey through it, and has a lot of very fun ideas, and lots of slimes. Tons of slimes.

  11. The Saint's magic power is omnipotent

    12. This anime follows Sei, a corporate drone who got summoned to another world along with Aria, who the prince who oversaw the summoning of them assumes is the saint. She learns potion making and confirms she is, in fact, the saint. It's a good anime with a fun storyline.

  12. Villainess level 99: I may be the hidden boss, but i'm not the demon lord

    13. Another villainess anime, this anime follows a girl reincarnated into a Otome Rpg game as the hidden boss. After being reincarnated, she gets distracted by the dungeon clearing aspect of the game and ends up becoming level 99 before the entering the academy. This causes a lot of shenanigains. It's a fun idea that's executed well.

  13. Unaware Atelier meister

    14. This anime is about a boy from a wandering village that doesn't have the same common sense as the rest of the world. He is rank f in all combat skills, but is SSS in all prodution skills.

  14. Bofuri: I don't want to get hurt so i'll max out my defence
    15. This anime is about a girl who was recomended a MMORPG by her best friend. Once she started playing, she chose a minmaxed defence build so she wouldn't get hurt. Through a series of lucky coincidenses and developer oversites, she along with her freind become some of the strongest players in the game.
